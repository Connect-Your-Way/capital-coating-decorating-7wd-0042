About us

Canberra's coating & decorating team.

Capital Coating & Decorating is a Canberra-based painting, plastering and decorating business working across the ACT. We take on the full surface job — preparation, plastering, painting, texture coating, wallpaper, rendering and decorative flooring — so homes and businesses get a complete, well-finished result from one team.

We focus on doing the groundwork right: surfaces prepared and repaired before the final coat goes on, work carried out punctually, and sites left clean. Whether it's a single room, a whole house repaint or a commercial floor, we're happy to talk through what your project needs.
